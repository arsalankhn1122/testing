About the model

Least-cost (cost-distance) model in ArcGIS Pro. Road speeds: car 30–100 km/h, bike 15 km/h, walk 5 km/h; off-road walking 3 km/h. Cost = 1 ÷ speed; Distance Accumulation from 109 hospitals and clinics. Model cells 50 m; this map shows a 200 m grid. No traffic or public transport included.
